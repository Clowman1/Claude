EQUITY BUYOUT AGREEMENT

(Release of Interest by Quitclaim Deed)

This Equity Buyout Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made and entered into as of the day of , 20 (the “Effective Date”), by and between WILLIAM MERREL STAINTON and LAMARCUS EDWARDS BARR STAINTON a/k/a MARK EDWARDS BARR STAINTON (each a “Seller” and collectively the “Sellers”), and WILLIAM EDWARDS ROBERTS (the “Buyer”). The Sellers and the Buyer are each referred to herein as a “Party” and collectively as the “Parties.”

Recitals

A. The Parties are the current record owners of that certain real property, together with all improvements thereon and all appurtenant condominium rights, commonly known as 2450 Gulf Boulevard, Unit 3B, Belleair Beach, Florida 33786, located in Pinellas County, Florida, and more particularly described in Exhibit A attached hereto (the “Property”).

B. Title to the Property is presently vested in the Parties pursuant to a Warranty Deed recorded in Official Records Book , Page , or as Instrument No. , of the Public Records of Pinellas County, Florida (the “Warranty Deed”).

C. The Sellers desire to release and convey all of their right, title, and interest in and to the Property to the Buyer, and the Buyer desires to acquire such interest and retain sole ownership of the Property, upon the terms and conditions set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Conveyance by Quitclaim Deed

At Closing (as defined below), each Seller shall execute, acknowledge, and deliver a Quitclaim Deed in recordable form conveying, remising, and releasing unto the Buyer all of such Seller’s right, title, and interest in and to the Property. Upon recording of the Quitclaim Deed, the Sellers shall be removed from title as vested under the Warranty Deed, and the Buyer shall retain and hold sole fee simple title to the Property.

3. Closing

The closing of the transaction contemplated herein (the “Closing”) shall take place on or before , or such other date as the Parties may mutually agree in writing, through as closing and escrow agent. At Closing, the Buyer shall deliver the Buyout Amount in immediately available funds, and the Sellers shall deliver the executed Quitclaim Deed(s) together with such affidavits and instruments as the closing agent may reasonably require.

4. Existing Mortgage

The existing mortgage lien encumbering the Property shall be satisfied in full at Closing in the amounts reflected on the payoff statement obtained by the closing agent. Upon such satisfaction, the Sellers shall have no further liability for said indebtedness. Should the final payoff figure differ from the figure used in Section 2, the Buyout Amount shall be adjusted accordingly on the closing statement, which shall be approved in writing by all Parties.

5. Taxes, Assessments, and Closing Costs

Real property taxes, condominium association assessments, and documentary stamp taxes have been allocated as set forth in Section 2. From and after Closing, the Buyer shall be solely responsible for all real property taxes, condominium assessments, insurance, utilities, and other charges relating to the Property. Except as expressly provided herein, each Party shall bear its own attorneys’ fees and costs.

6. Representations of the Sellers

Each Seller represents that such Seller has not conveyed, encumbered, or otherwise transferred any interest in the Property, other than as reflected in the public records, and has full authority to execute this Agreement and the Quitclaim Deed. The Buyer acknowledges that the Quitclaim Deed conveys only such interest as the Sellers may hold, without warranty of title, and that the Buyer accepts the Property in its present “as is” condition.

7. Mutual Release

Effective upon Closing and receipt of the Buyout Amount by the Sellers, each Party releases and forever discharges the other Parties from any and all claims, demands, and obligations arising from their co-ownership of the Property, excepting only the obligations created by this Agreement. The Buyer shall indemnify and hold the Sellers harmless from any liability relating to the ownership, use, or operation of the Property arising after Closing.

8. General Provisions

(a) Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding of the Parties regarding its subject matter and may be amended only by a written instrument signed by all Parties. (b) Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Florida, and venue for any action arising hereunder shall lie in Pinellas County, Florida. (c) Binding Effect. This Agreement shall bind and benefit the Parties and their respective heirs, personal representatives, successors, and assigns. (d) Counterparts; Electronic Signatures. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together shall constitute one instrument. (e) Independent Counsel. Each Party acknowledges that they have had the opportunity to consult independent legal and tax counsel prior to executing this Agreement.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Equity Buyout Agreement as of the Effective Date.
