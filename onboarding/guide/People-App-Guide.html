1. What People is, and who can use it

People replaces the onboarding spreadsheet. Every employee has one permanent record, and that record carries checklists for each stage of employment:

ChecklistWhat it coversDue dates count from
OnboardingThe 53-step new-hire list: pre-start, Day 1, Week 1, 30 and 90 daysThe start date
Annual ComplianceLicense renewals per state, NMLS CE, BSA/AML, Fair Lending, GLBA privacy, IT security, handbookDecember 31 of the year
OffboardingResignation paperwork through equipment return, access shutdown, NMLS removal and COBRAThe last day
BranchOpening a new branch: approval, licensing, setup and launchThe target open date

Every task records who completed it and when. A finished checklist is signed off, which locks it and files a regulator-format PDF. That's the record examiners ask for.

Access: only Christopher Lowman, Bianca Rodrigues and Adam Stephens can open People. Access comes from the People Admin permission set. Nobody else sees the tab, the records or the HR data. Employees don't need a Salesforce login to be managed in People.

Reading a card

  • The ring shows the percentage of tasks done. The pill shows status (Candidate, Pre-Start, Active, Offboarding).
  • Next: the next open task and its due date. Overdue (amber) counts tasks past their due date.
  • Click a card to open that person's page with that checklist showing.

The digest panel and the missing-info banner

  • Amber banner: counts employees with required information missing. Review opens the list on the Compliance tab.

4. Onboarding a new hire

1On the Onboarding tab, click + New Employee.

2Fill in the form. First name, last name, role, work arrangement and start date are required. The rest can be added later:

FieldNotes
RoleLoan Officer, Branch Manager, Processor, Closer, Opener, Operations/Admin, Manager. Sets which tasks apply.
Work arrangementIn-Office, Remote, Hybrid. VPN only for Remote/Hybrid; desk and business cards only for In-Office.
Start dateEvery due date is calculated from this.
Title, department, employment type, schedule, hire dateHR details (section 5).
Branch, manager, emails, NMLS IDPersonal email is where the onboarding emails go.
Licensed statesCreates a Pending license for each state (section 7).

3Before you save, the blue box previews the result. For example: "50 tasks will be generated for a Loan Officer (Remote), plus 1 optional to add. First task due Mon, Nov 9."

4Click Create and generate tasks. The employee page opens with the checklist ready.

How due dates are worked out

  • Most tasks count business days from the start date and skip weekends and company holidays. For example, "−14" means 14 business days before Day 1.
  • The benefits deadline, 30-day check-in, scheduling the 90-day, and the 90-day check-in count calendar days. If one lands on a weekend or holiday it moves to the next business day.
  • Moving the start date (Edit details) re-dates every open task. Completed tasks keep their dates, and you're warned if any open task now falls in the past.

Status moves on its own

Candidate → Pre-Start when "Collect background check info" (PH-4) is done → Active on the start date (overnight job) → Offboarding → Terminated when the offboarding checklist is signed off. Withdraw (employee page) is for a candidate who backs out or fails the background check: open tasks become N/A and the history is kept.

Completing, editing and adding tasks

  • Tick the box to complete a task. It's stamped with your name and the time. Unticking reopens it, until the checklist is signed off.
  • Pencil (✎): change the assignee (Chris, Bianca or Adam), the due date or notes, or Mark N/A if the task doesn't apply.
  • Chips: Led by shows who actually runs the task (e.g. Angelina), DocuSign means it's sent through DocuSign, To: shows the recipient, and Email reminder marks a task that sends reminders when emails are on.
  • Optional items: tasks that only apply sometimes (e.g. FHA Connection ID). Click Add to checklist when needed.
  • Add a one-off task: a task for this person only, with subject, phase, due date and assignee.

Sending the onboarding emails (PH-3, PH-4, PH-7, PH-8)

  1. Click Send PH-x on the task. A preview opens, already filled in with the hire's name, NMLS ID and the Paychex "2 business days" date.
  2. Check or edit the To, Subject and message. The email ends with your own signature card (new logo, headshot, title).
  3. Click Send. It goes from your email address, the task is marked done, and a note records who it went to and when.

PH-6, PH-9, PH-10, Day 1 Instructions and Team Announcement go through DocuSign, so tick those tasks by hand once sent. The template wording can be edited in Setup → Classic Email Templates → folder People Onboarding.

Compliance sign-off and the exam PDF

  • When every task is done or N/A, Sign off unlocks for Bianca, with Chris as backup. You confirm the attestation: "I attest that every item on this checklist was completed as recorded."
  • Signing off locks the checklist and files the exam PDF on the employee's record: header, items grouped Regulatory / Systems / Documentation, completed-by and date for each, and the sign-off block. Open filed PDF opens it.

Licenses: one per state

  • Add license or click a license to edit its state, license number, status (Active / Pending / Inactive / Expired), sponsorship (Sponsored / Pending / Removed), issue date, expiration date and last renewed date.
  • The person's Licensed States are worked out from their Active and Pending licenses and copied to their Salesforce user, which LO Workspace and signature cards use. You don't type states anywhere else.
  • When someone stops holding a state, set it to Inactive rather than deleting it.
  • Ticking "Confirm {state} renewal approved" in Annual Compliance marks that license renewed today, valid through December 31 next year.
  • The NMLS ID is kept on the employee record (Edit details) and copied to their Salesforce user.
All 32 current licenses were created without numbers or expiration dates, so licensed staff appear under Missing information until Bianca fills them in.

Compensation: dated history

  • Add entry for each pay change: effective date and pay type (required), base amount per year or hour, LO comp in bps, a per-loan flat amount, bonus plan and notes.
  • The newest entry is marked Current. For a raise, add a new entry rather than editing the old one, so the history stays intact. Delete only mistakes.
  • Only the three People admins can see compensation in People. Full Salesforce System Administrators can technically read it as well.

9. Offboarding

1Open the employee (from Staff), click Offboard, and enter the last day and reason (Resigned / Terminated / Other).

2Their status becomes Offboarding and a 13-task checklist is dated from the last day:

PhaseTasks
Before last dayResignation letter or separation agreement · notice of termination filed · reassign open Leads and Transactions · FedEx return slip (remote/hybrid)
Last dayEquipment and keys · deactivate Salesforce user · disable ARIVE, Encompass, UWM/EASE, Advantage, CIC, RingCentral, GoHighLevel, Trainual, DocuSign · KB Technologies Outlook / M365 shutdown · final pay in Paychex
After last dayRemove from NMLS (licensed) · remove from Teams and distribution lists · COBRA paperwork · confirm equipment received

3An amber line on their page shows what they still have: "To hand over: 4 open Leads owned and 2 active Transactions as Loan Officer."

4Sign off the offboarding checklist when it's complete. The employee becomes Terminated, and their history stays on file.

11. Staff and adding existing employees

The Staff tab lists everyone who works here: name, role, status, start date, manager, NMLS ID and states. Rows with an Needs info marker are missing required information. Click any row to open the person.

Adding someone who already works here

Many employees don't use Salesforce. To add one:

  1. On Staff, click + Add existing employee.
  2. Enter first and last name and role (required), plus title, department, employment type, schedule, hire date, branch, manager, emails, NMLS ID and licensed states.
  3. Salesforce user is optional. Leave it empty for people without a login.
  4. Click Add employee. They're created Active, with no onboarding checklist and an Active license for each state. During renewal season their Annual Compliance opens straight away.
When a Salesforce user is created later with the same email as someone's work email, People links them automatically and ticks "Set up Salesforce user".
