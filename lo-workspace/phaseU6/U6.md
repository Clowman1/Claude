## Changes
- **New `LoWsVolumeService.getVolume()`.** These figures run in user mode:
  - Closed MTD and last month: Transactions with Loan_Officer__c or OwnerId = me, by `Funding_Date__c` (MTD stops at today), `SUM(Loan_Amount_1st__c)` plus count. Cancelled and Lost are excluded.
  - Pre-approved: my unconverted Leads in status Pre-Approval, `SUM(Loan_Amount__c)` plus count.
  - New leads: Leads I own created this month and last month.
  - Realtor partners added: my `External_User_Assignment__c` tags (Assigned_Date__c, else CreatedDate) this month and last month, where the partner's BD_Industry__c is Realtor or blank. Other industries are U7's Other Contacts. This one query runs in system mode, scoped to User__c = me.
- **New LWC `loWsVolumeDrawer`**: fixed "Volume" tab on the right edge (top 160px, clear of the utility bar) and a 360px slide-out drawer laid out like the approved mockup. It reloads the figures each time it opens; Esc, ×, or a click on the dimmed page closes it.
- **`externalLoPortalShell`** renders the drawer only in LO Workspace (not the portal) on Perfect Day, My Loans, Pre-Approvals, My Leads and My Realtors. U7 adds Other Contacts.
- `External_LO_Perfect_Day_Access` grants the class.
- When U8 lands: exclude recycled leads from the new-lead count (marked `ponytail:` in the service).
