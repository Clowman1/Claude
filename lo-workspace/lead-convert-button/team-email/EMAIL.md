**Subject:** New: Convert to Transaction button on the Lead

Hi team,

Starting today, there's a new way to convert a lead into a loan. Use the **Convert to Transaction** button at the top of the Lead in Salesforce, in the Lead Actions bar (screenshot 1).

**When you'll see it**
- The lead's status is **Pre-Approval** or **Under Contract**
- You're the lead's **Owner or Loan Partner**

**How it works**
1. Click **Convert to Transaction**. A checklist of the TRID application pieces opens (screenshot 2).
2. Anything already in Salesforce from ARIVE shows a green check. For anything missing, either fill it in on the lead or click **Info received** if you have it.
3. Tick the confirmation box and click **Confirm conversion**. Salesforce converts the lead, creates the Transaction and takes you straight to it. Notes and co-borrowers carry over.
4. Click **Decline** to back out. The lead stays unchanged.

**If it says "This lead can't convert yet"** (screenshot 3), the lead still has open document work, and nothing on the lead has changed. The message lists each item by name:
- **Uploaded documents waiting for your decision.** Go to the **Documents** tab and approve or reject each one.
- **Open conditions still owed by the borrower.** Go to the **Document requests** tab and clear, approve or complete each one.

Once every item is done, click Convert to Transaction again.

This is the same conversion screen our outside LOs already use in the LO Workspace, so we're all on one process now.
