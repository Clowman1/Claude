# Phase U7 – Other Contacts tab + Volume drawer v2 (LO Workspace upgrades Oct 2026)

Retrieved fresh from prod 2026-10-08 (after U6); `ref/` = untouched prod copies.

## Other Contacts
- **`ExternalLoPortalService.getPartners(filterKey, search, kind)`**. In LO Workspace tag mode only, partners are split by BD_Industry__c:
  - `realtor`: Realtor or blank.
  - `other`: every other industry.
  - `all`: both.
  - Partner Workspace ignores kind and is unchanged.
- `getRealtors` = kind `realtor`: My Realtors, Realtor Prospects and the "due realtors" badge are now realtors only.
- The Perfect Day "partners due" record set uses `all`.
- New controller method `getPartnersByKind`.
- **`externalLoRealtors`** gains `@api contactKind`. With `other` it shows:
  - Title "Other Contacts" and its own subtitle.
  - Its own navigation view, so detail/back/filter stay on Other Contacts.
  - No "New lead" row action.
  - Partner type picker without Realtor, defaulting to Insurance Agent.
- `portalUtils`: new view `OTHER_CONTACTS` ('otherContacts'). The shell adds an "Other Contacts" nav item (LO Workspace only, handshake icon) under My Realtors and renders the component there.
- **BD_Industry__c** gets *Home Inspector* and *Appraiser*. Five record types are redeployed with the values: Business_Account plus PersonAccount, Recruit_Account, Candidate and BizDev_Expansion. This is the picklist / record-type rule.
- `LoWsPartnerTagServiceTest`: tag tests use `getPartnersByKind(…, 'all')`. Realtor Prospects now excludes the title agent; the title agent appears in Other Contacts.

## Volume drawer v2 (user request mid-U7)
- `LoWsVolumeService` adds firstName, photoUrl, dayOfMonth and daysInMonth. Last-month label is the full month name.
- `loWsVolumeDrawer` redesign:
  - Blue-to-navy edge tab with a trend icon.
  - Navy gradient header: "Good morning, <first name>", "Your <Month> volume", photo or initial.
  - Large MTD closed figure.
  - Pace bar: MTD as a % of last month's total, with a marker for today's position in the month.
  - Tinted body with colour-coded icon tiles: closed last month (navy), pre-approved (green), new leads (amber), realtor partners (violet).
- The drawer also shows on Other Contacts. Mockup screen 3 updated to match.
