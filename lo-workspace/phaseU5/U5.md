# Phase U5 – Communications tab (LO Workspace upgrades Oct 2026)

Retrieved fresh from prod 2026-10-08 (after U4 and the board-label hotfix); `ref/` = untouched prod copies.

## Changes
- **New `LoWsCommunicationsService.getCommunications(recordId)`** (AuraEnabled, cacheable).
  - First `RecordAccessGuard.requireRead`, then SYSTEM_MODE reads scoped to one borrower.
  - The borrower is resolved from a Lead, Transaction__c (via Borrower_Name__c) or Person Account. It includes the leads converted into that account and its loans, so history follows conversion.
  - Phones are normalised to E.164 to match RingCentral. The na@na.com placeholder is never matched.
  - **Calls / SMS**: Tasks linked to any of those records, plus RingCentral Tasks (last 365 days) whose from/to number is the borrower's. Hand-logged calls (Activity_Type__c Call – … / Text, TaskSubtype Call) are tagged "Logged". Direction comes from the from-number or Direction__c. A "missed" in the Subject marks a missed call.
  - **RingSense summaries**: Call_Insight__c rows attach to the call within 5 minutes; unmatched insights are listed as their own call.
  - **Emails**: EmailMessageRelation on those records or the borrower's addresses, grouped by ThreadIdentifier (or subject without Re:/Fw:). Bodies are clipped at 4,000 characters; at most 150 emails and 300 calls/texts.
  - Every Reach user's communications are shown, labelled by sender.
- **New LWC `loWsCommunications`**: Calls / SMS / Emails tabs with counts.
  - Calls: direction icon, "From → To", date and duration, Summary toggle.
  - SMS: day-grouped bubbles, borrower on the left.
  - Emails: collapsible threads, newest open.
  - Read-only.
- Tab added to the **Lead** and **Loan** drawers (LO Workspace only, `internalOnly`), the **Past Client** page, and the **LO_Workspace_Account_Record_Page** flexipage (new "Communications" tab after RingSense).
- `External_LO_Perfect_Day_Access` grants the new class.
