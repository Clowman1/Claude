# Phase U3 – Weekly touch + Up Next "Open all" (LO Workspace upgrades Oct 2026)

Retrieved fresh from prod 2026-10-08 (after U2); `ref/` = untouched prod copies.

## Changes
- **New `LoWsTouchService`.** A touch is any of these, looked back over 90 days:
  - A completed Task where TaskSubtype is Call/Email, or InteractionType__c is Call/Sms, or Type is Call, or Activity_Type__c is set (hand-logged).
  - An Event on the lead.
  - Any `EmailMessageRelation` on the lead, or to/from its address (case-insensitive).
  - Due = 7+ days since the last touch. With no touch, the clock starts at Pre_Approval_Date__c, then CreatedDate.
- **Pre-approvals list:**
  - Status is now *Weekly touch due* or *On track*. The old "No contact 6+ days" (RingCentral fields only) is removed.
  - Touch-due rows sort first, longest without a touch at the top, then newest created.
  - The row shows "Last touch <date>" or "No touch yet".
- **Perfect Day:**
  - The "Pre-approvals need review" card counts touch-due leads.
  - Up Next gets "Weekly touch: <name>" prompts, at most 3 in the capped card list, ahead of the computed prompts. Log and Tomorrow work as usual.
- **Open all** (`PerfectDayHomeController.getAllUpNext` → `ExternalLoPromptService.getAllUpNext`): uncapped.
  - Overdue / Today: document reviews, due follow-ups, weekly touches and computed prompts.
  - Coming up: open Tasks and Events in the next 7 days.
  - The modal in `externalLoActivityPrompts` has filters All / Weekly touches / Tasks / Appointments. A row opens the Lead, Realtor or Loan in the workspace.
- `LeadRepository` pre-approval selects add Email and CreatedDate and drop Credit_Expiration_Date__c.
- `PerfectDayHomeServiceTest` query budget raised from 16 to 19: three fixed touch queries, still flat with volume.
