# Phase U2 – My Loans tabs + board (LO Workspace upgrades Oct 2026)

Retrieved fresh from prod 2026-10-08 (after U1); `ref/` = untouched prod copies.

## Changes
- **Tabs**: the filter chips are now *My Active Loans / My Closed Loans / My Cancelled Loans*, with counts from the new `getLoanTabCounts`.
  - Active = `ACTIVE_LOAN_STATUSES` minus Funding and PC Review.
  - Closed = Funding, PC Review, Post-Closed (newest closing first).
  - Cancelled = Cancelled, Lost (newest first).
  - Old links (`all`, `update`, `processing`, `ctc`, e.g. the Perfect Day drawer) land on Active.
- **Board** (Active tab, default): columns Application / Processing / Approved / Clear to Close / Closing, from `ExternalLoPortalService.boardStage`.
  - The Closing status wins, then a Clear to Close date, then the status group.
  - Cards flagged "Needs update" (Waiting On Loan Officer) get a red edge.
  - The Board/List toggle sits in the header; the choice is kept in the browser's localStorage (`loWs.myLoans.view`).
- `LoanRepository.selectPortalLoansByUser(userId, statuses, newestFirst)` and `countPortalLoansByUser` added; the old one-arg method is unchanged.
- **Shared `portalListLayout`**: new optional `custom-body` + `body` slot. It's additive, and no other list uses it.
- The "loans in process" badge (`getLoans('all')`) now excludes Funding and PC Review, matching the Active tab.
